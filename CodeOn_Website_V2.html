About CodeOn

We exist because
the world needs builders.

CodeOn was built on one belief — that structured, disciplined, community-driven growth produces the kind of people who change the world. Not eventually. Now.

The movement.

CodeOn was founded on the understanding that talent is everywhere — but structure, community and recognition are not. Too many brilliant minds work alone, grow without direction and achieve without acknowledgement. CodeOn changes that.

Every CodeOn chapter is a living, breathing engine of growth. Members bring real problems. Members build real solutions. Members present, challenge, evaluate and grow together — week after week, month after month, year after year. This is not a club you join. It is a standard you rise to.

The movement is designed to outlast any one person. It runs on a system — elected leadership, structured meetings, a universal charter — that means any chapter anywhere in the world operates at the same level of excellence. That is what makes it global.

The Core Values

What we stand for.

Value 01

Proof Over Promise

Every level, every title, every recognition in CodeOn is backed by real evidence. What you build, what you solve, what you prove. Words without results mean nothing here.

Value 02

Community is the Engine

CodeOn does not grow from the top down. It grows because members bring others, chapters birth chapters, and every person who rises makes space for the next. The community builds itself.

Value 03

Local Problems. Global Standards.

The problems CodeOn solves are rooted in the communities members live in. The standard of how they are solved is world class. Both things are true at once. That is the CodeOn way.

Value 04

Growth Never Stops

There is no finish line in CodeOn. A Legend still competes. A Pioneer still builds. The system is designed so that at every level, there is always something worth chasing — always a reason to keep going.

The Levels System

Seven levels.
A lifetime of growth.

Every level in CodeOn is earned — not given, not bought, not voted on. Time, real projects and proven tests. The value of each level is protected by what it truly costs to reach it.

Earned by time served Earned by real projects Earned by rigorous tests Never by votes Never by shortcuts Self-paced, not rushed
Level 01
✦
Spark

Who you are

You have arrived. You have found people who think the way you think, who care about what you care about, who want to build the kind of things you want to build. You are learning the fundamentals — the language, the structure, the CodeOn way. This is where every great member begins.

What you need to move forward

Complete orientationAttend minimum meetingsPass basic technical test
Level 02
⚙️
Builder

Who you are

You have stopped just learning and started making. Your first real projects exist in the world. You present at meetings with confidence. You contribute to community problem sessions with ideas that have weight. You are building things — and people are starting to notice.

What you need to move forward

Minimum time servedFirst real project completedIntermediate test passed
Level 03
🔍
Solver

Who you are

You are no longer building for the sake of building. You are solving real problems that real people have. Your work has touched your community. Something that existed wrong now exists right because of you. That is the Solver standard — and it is not easily met.

What you need to move forward

Minimum time servedCommunity problem solvedSolver test passed
Level 04
💡
Innovator

Who you are

You have built something original — something that did not exist before you conceived it. Not an improvement on what was there. Something new. Innovators in CodeOn are rare because this level demands genuine creativity backed by real execution. If you are here, you have earned respect.

What you need to move forward

Minimum time servedOriginal innovation builtLeadership and tech test
Level 05
🚀
Pioneer

Who you are

You have started something new. A chapter that did not exist — built, structured, running and growing because of your initiative. Pioneers are the reason CodeOn grows. Without them, the movement stops at one chapter. With them, it reaches every corner of the world.

What you need to move forward

Minimum time servedNew chapter successfully startedPioneer assessment completed

🏛️
Architect

Who you are

You are not just a member of CodeOn anymore — you are part of what CodeOn is. Architects shape the structure, refine the system, and make decisions that affect chapters across the world. Your contribution is structural. Your legacy is built into the foundation of the movement itself.

What you need to move forward

Minimum time servedStructural contribution to CodeOnPanel review completed

A Legend is not announced. A Legend is recognised. After a lifetime of building, solving, innovating, pioneering and architecting — the movement itself decides that your contribution has permanently changed what CodeOn is. You are part of CodeOn's history. You will always be. This is the highest honour the movement can give — and it cannot be taken away.

The Titles System

Honours that keep
everyone hungry.

Titles run parallel to levels. They can be won at any level, at any time. A Spark can hold a title that a Legend has not earned. Nobody in CodeOn is ever complete. Nobody ever stops competing.

🏆
Competition Titles
Won at CodeOn Hackathons

Competition Titles are the purest form of recognition CodeOn offers. They are awarded at chapter, regional, national and Grand hackathon levels — and they are entirely objective. You built something. It was judged. You won. Nobody can argue with that. A Spark who outbuilds every Innovator in the room takes home what they earned and no one can take it from them.

What earns a Competition Title

  • Winning at a CodeOn Chapter Hackathon
  • Winning at a Regional Hackathon
  • Winning at a National Hackathon
  • Winning the CodeOn Grand Hackathon
Difficulty
🤝
Community Titles
Voted by Chapter Members

Community Titles cannot be won by being the best coder in the room. They are voted on by the people who work alongside you — the members who have watched you show up, lift others, mentor without being asked and make the chapter feel like the family it is supposed to be. You can be the most brilliant person in CodeOn and never earn this. It requires something different. Something deeper.

What earns a Community Title

  • Exceptional mentorship of other members
  • Consistent contribution to chapter culture
  • Demonstrated service beyond personal gain
  • A vote of genuine respect from peers
Difficulty
🌟
Innovation Titles
Real World Proof Required

Innovation Titles are the hardest to earn in all of CodeOn. A plan does not qualify. A prototype does not qualify. A pitch does not qualify. What qualifies is a project that is running in the real world and demonstrably improving real lives — with evidence. The number of people it reached. The problem it eliminated. The change it caused. If you hold an Innovation Title, you have changed something that needed changing. That is permanent.

What earns an Innovation Title

  • A live project solving a verified real-world problem
  • Measurable impact with documented evidence
  • Review and confirmation by the CodeOn chapter
  • A result — not a plan, not a prototype
Difficulty

1
Find a Chapter

Use the chapter directory below or email us at codeon.ug@gmail.com to find the nearest active chapter to you.

Start a Chapter

If you are a Pioneer or above, you have the right to start a CodeOn chapter anywhere in the world. Here is how.

1
Download the Starter Kit

The Chapter Starter Kit contains the Charter, Meeting Manual, Organisation Structure and Founding Guide — everything you need.

2
Find Your First 10

Identify and personally invite 10 founding members. These are your Founding Sparks — their title is permanent from day one.

3
Register Your Chapter

Contact CodeOn at codeon.ug@gmail.com to register your chapter officially. Your chapter goes into the global Registry.

4
Run Your First Meeting

Follow the Meeting Manual exactly. Take the group photo. The chapter is alive. Everything else grows from that first room.

Hackathons

Build under pressure.
Win with proof.

CodeOn hackathons are not showcases. They are battles — timed, judged and decided by what you build and what it does. Every level of competition leads to the next. The best rise. No exceptions.

Stage 01
🏠
Chapter Hackathon
Internal competition within your chapter. All members eligible. Winners earn a Competition Title and advance.
→
Stage 02
🌍
Regional Hackathon
Chapter winners compete across chapters in the region. Higher stakes, broader problems, stronger competition.
→
Stage 03
🏳️
National Hackathon
The best regional teams compete at country level. A national Competition Title is one of CodeOn's most respected honours.
→
Stage 04
🌐
CodeOn Grand
The global stage. National winners. The Grand Competition Title. The highest competitive honour CodeOn can give.
🥇
Grand Champion

The Grand Competition Title. Permanent. Global. The single most prestigious competitive honour in CodeOn — won at the Grand Hackathon by the team that outbuilt everyone else on the world stage.

🥈
National Champion

Won at the CodeOn National Hackathon. A national Competition Title that marks you as the best builder in your country at that moment. A title that opens every door.

🎖️
Chapter Champion

Every competition begins here. The Chapter Competition Title is the first honour a member can earn in competition — and the springboard to everything above it.

Members fund their own journey.

Participation in CodeOn hackathons is self-funded by members — because those who invest in their own journey compete with everything they have. Sponsorships and prizes grow as CodeOn grows.
