## The Three Arms of CodeOn

### Arm 01: Student Dev Agency (`agency.html`)
- **What it is**: A working digital dev studio staffed by CodeOn's strongest student developers. It takes on real client work under the same discovery-to-delivery process a professional studio runs.
- **Leadership**: Led by **Justin**, Agency Manager.
- **What they build**:
  - Web platforms and applications
  - Mobile applications & MVPs
  - Internal business tools and automation systems
  - Production-standard digital solutions
- **How they work (Three-Step Process)**:
  1. **Step 01 - Discovery**: Scoping the real problem with the client — requirements, constraints, success metrics before writing code.
  2. **Step 02 - Build**: Development in short, reviewed sprint cycles with Justin overseeing quality, architecture, and pace.
  3. **Step 03 - Deliver**: A working, well-documented product plus an ongoing team that understands the codebase to support future iterations.
- **Client Route**:
  - Direct inquiry email: `info@codeondigital.com`
  - Scoping questions to ask clients: What are you building (web platform, mobile app, internal tool, MVP)? What is your expected timeline? What is your budget range?
  - Rule: Never invent prices, hourly rates, or fixed delivery dates. Direct clients to submit an inquiry or email `info@codeondigital.com`.

### Arm 02: Schools & Outreach Program — "Build. Solve. Belong." (`outreach.html`)
- **What it is**: CodeOn's school outreach and certification program. Brings hands-on coding literacy, purpose, and community directly into primary and secondary classrooms across Kampala and the region.
- **Leadership**: Led by **Evelyne** and **Ramathan**, Outreach Leads.
- **Key Highlights**:
  - Hands-on sessions: Students work on real laptops, write real code, and achieve immediate small wins.
  - Clear certification pathway: A structured progression that students can carry forward into further education and tech careers.
  - Built around belonging: Fosters peer connection, confidence, and community rather than rote theoretical curriculum.
  - Real classroom track record: Hundreds of students reached, including sessions at schools such as Ebenezer Children's Centre and community spaces across Kampala.
- **School Administrator / Teacher Route**:
  - Direct inquiry email: `school@codeondigital.com`
  - Scoping questions to ask schools: School name? District / Location in Uganda? Student age group / grade level? Preferred school term or timing for a visit?
  - Rule: Never invent session fees or fixed schedules. Offer to submit a school visit request or email `school@codeondigital.com`.

### Arm 03: Tech Events & Hackathons (`events.html`)
- **What it is**: Hackathons, technical workshops, and community gatherings that bring student builders, schools, industry mentors, and diplomatic partners into one room.
- **Leadership**: Led by **Humphrey** and **Josephyne**, Event Directors.
- **Key Events & Partners**:
  - **U.S. Embassy Uganda / American Center Kampala**: Regular dev workshops and collaboration sessions hosted at the American Center in Kampala.
  - **Hack 4 Bodas — AI for Smart City Mobility**: Kampala hackathon focused on building AI solutions with Google's Gemma model to tackle motorcycle taxi (boda boda) urban mobility and safety challenges.
  - **Build with AI — Makerere Hackathon**: CodeOn team entry and participation in Makerere University developer hackathons.
  - **CodeOn Team Culture Day**: Community gathering, merchandise launch, and all-hands team onboarding.
- **Partnership & Student Route**:
  - Direct partnership email: `chapter@codeondigital.com`
  - For students wishing to join: Attend upcoming events, join local chapters, and engage with the community.
  - For event sponsors/venues: Email `chapter@codeondigital.com` to host, sponsor, or co-run events.
